## Meet Herm & Essie
### LittLEMorE AI Studio

A warm, vintage-inspired recording studio in development, brought to life by two distinctive sound-engineer characters.

The current prototype includes recording, in-desk playback, effects and a drum sequencer. The planned AI engineer experience and commercial release are still in development.
